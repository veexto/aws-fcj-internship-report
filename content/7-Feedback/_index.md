### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
The working atmosphere at both the AWS office and across the online channels of the First Cloud Journey (FCJ) community is relaxed, open, and welcoming. People do not get bogged down by rigid formalities; instead, everyone focuses on technical discussions and helping each other solve problems. Adding a few casual networking sessions or smaller topic-based discussion groups (such as Security or AI/Data tracks) would make it even easier for interns to break the ice and connect.

**2. Support from Mentors / Team Admin**  
What I appreciate most about the mentors is their thought-provoking guidance: whenever I ran into a bug or hit a roadblock, they would ask guiding questions so I could trace the root cause myself before offering optimization tips. Alongside that, the admin team was super supportive—from provisioning lab accounts and sending worklog reminders to sharing event updates—so I never felt lost throughout the internship.

**3. Relevance of Work to My Major**  
The tasks I took on during the internship—ranging from building architectures on AWS and translating technical blogs to writing workshop guides—aligned closely with my university major. By configuring services and troubleshooting real errors hands-on, I gained a much deeper grasp of concepts I had previously only studied in theory, especially cost optimization and security.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**  
Writing weekly worklogs consistently and presenting technical concepts through bilingual blogs helped me build a habit of structured documentation and clearer communication. Beyond technical skills, the practical career talks from industry speakers gave me a much clearer picture of my career direction after graduation.

**5. Company Culture & Team Spirit**  
The spirit of sharing at FCJ stands out clearly. Whether someone is a beginner or an experienced engineer, everyone communicates openly and respectfully. Whenever project deadlines got tight or I ran into tricky configuration issues, posting a message in the group always brought teammates willing to jump in and help.

**6. Internship Policies & Benefits**  
The program offers a flexible schedule that made it easy to balance university classes with internship milestones. Getting hands-on AWS lab resources and free access to in-office workshops and Watch Parties was a huge plus for students.

### Additional Questions

**1. What are you most satisfied with during your internship?**  
I am most satisfied with learning in an environment that combines discipline with the freedom to experiment and learn from mistakes. Instead of being restricted to dry theoretical tasks, I got to build real solutions, write real technical documentation, and receive close, constructive feedback from mentors along the way.

**2. What areas should the company improve for future internship batches?**  
I think the program could add a mid-term architecture review session (around Week 6 or Week 7) for project teams. Getting early feedback from mentors at that stage would give us enough time to refine our designs before entering the final sprint.

**3. Would you recommend this internship program to your friends? Why?**  
Definitely. For students who want to take Cloud and AI seriously, this is a very practical place to start. You get hands-on experience directly on AWS while sharpening your technical writing, teamwork, and networking with talented peers and industry professionals.

---

### Suggestions & Expectations

**Do you have any suggestions to enhance the internship experience?**  
I hope that in the future, there will be even more activities where students can showcase their talents, along with organized competitions aimed at encouraging everyone to share and contribute their knowledge—creating a healthy, rewarding learning playground and community.

**Would you like to continue participating in the program in the future?**  
Yes. After completing my internship, I would love to stay active in the AWS Study Group and FCJ community to keep up with new technologies and support future batches of interns whenever I can.

**Additional Comments:**  
Thank you to all the mentors, the FCJ admin team, and my fellow interns for supporting me so much throughout the past three months.
